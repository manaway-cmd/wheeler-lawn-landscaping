Frequently asked questions

How much does sprinkler repair cost in Lee's Summit?

Most single-zone repairs, like a broken head or a stuck valve, run in the low hundreds including the trip. Bigger jobs, such as a mainline break or a controller replacement, cost more. We give a firm price after we see the system, not before.

When should I winterize my sprinkler system in the Kansas City area?

Get it done before the first hard freeze, typically mid-to-late October here. Water left in the pipes over winter expands as it freezes and cracks fittings and backflow devices underground, where you cannot see it happen.

When should I turn my sprinkler system on in spring?

Once overnight lows stay reliably above freezing, usually early-to-mid April in this area. Turning it on too early risks a late frost hitting water still in the lines. We check zones, heads, and pressure at start-up so nothing runs blind.
