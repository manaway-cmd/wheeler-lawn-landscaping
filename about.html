How we work

We answer the phone or call back the same day in most cases. No guesswork on price. We give a firm number after we've actually seen the system, and we use Hunter, Rain Bird, and Toro parts, since those are the brands most systems around here already run on.

On licensing

Missouri does not license irrigation contractors at the state level. Some states do — Texas issues an irrigator license with a number you can look up in a state database. Missouri handles irrigation through city permitting and general contractor rules instead, and those vary from one municipality to the next.

That's why there is no state license number anywhere on this site. There isn't one to show. Insurance is the separate question, and it's a fair one to ask before any crew digs in your yard — that detail is still unconfirmed above.

What we don't do

We don't push a full system replacement when a repair will hold. Full stop. We won't guess at a diagnosis without testing the zone first, and if a job falls outside what we handle, we say so instead of taking it anyway.
